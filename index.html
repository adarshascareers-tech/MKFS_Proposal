Included in every tier — done once at the start of the engagement, and refreshed periodically after.

Categories, service listings, photos, and business details set up to support local search and lead discovery.

LinkedIn profile optimization

How We'll Work

The process, either way

These apply across every tier — the only thing that changes by tier is how much flexibility is built in for revisions.

Two options, every post

Every post comes with two creative/caption directions to choose from, so you're picking, not waiting on a single draft to be right.

One week ahead, always

All content for the week is delivered 7 days in advance, so there's time to review, request changes, and approve before it's due to go live.

Revisions

Freelance (Tiers 01 & 02): up to 2 rounds of changes per piece.
Full-Time (Tier 03): no cap on revisions.

Paid Media

Ad budget, by platform

This is MKFS's media spend — separate from the management fee, which is already included in every tier above. The same ad creative runs across Facebook and Instagram as one Meta campaign, so there's no duplicate design cost. We start lean on one platform, prove it works, then add the next.

Platform Starting Budget Why
Meta (Facebook + Instagram) — Start here ₹5,000 – ₹8,000/month One creative, run across both placements. Lowest cost-per-click of the options, enough to test messaging without a large spend.
WhatsApp Marketing ₹0 to start Starts on the free WhatsApp Business App — broadcasts to up to 256 contacts, no monthly cost. Upgrades to a paid tool (₹1,000–₹2,500/month + ~₹0.86/message) only once volume outgrows that.
LinkedIn — Add next ₹6,000 – ₹10,000/month Higher CPC in finance/B2B, but reaches the business-owner audience MKFS is built for. Add once Meta shows what's working.
Combined, once all are running ₹11,000 – ₹18,000/month A realistic total with Meta, LinkedIn, and (if upgraded) paid WhatsApp live together.
Google Search — Phase 2 ₹6,000 – ₹10,000/month Add once Meta/LinkedIn show what messaging converts.
YouTube — Phase 2 ₹4,000 – ₹6,000/month Add once there's a library of video content worth promoting.
→

Starting point for the Growth tier: Meta (Facebook + Instagram) only, at ₹5,000–₹8,000/month, for the first 4–6 weeks. WhatsApp marketing runs on the free Business App from day one — no cost until message volume genuinely outgrows it. LinkedIn ads get added once we see what's converting on Meta.
